Long story short

I never used to cook. Back home, my parents did all the cooking for me, right up until I moved abroad.

In Singapore, cooking at home doesn't save you much compared to buying food. The harder part is eating healthy. Here (and in a lot of other countries), "healthy" is often a marketing angle, so it costs more. Still, you can find decent places with a good balance between cheap and healthy. With a busy schedule, I didn't cook for about four years. I just bought food from a few places I trusted.

Recently I started cooking. I call these my cooking experiments. I think like a designer, I build things as a developer, and I like to tinker. So I put meals together from very basic, easy building blocks that are healthy, tasty and quick to prep.

This page is where I'll share that food journey. Don't expect fancy or unique dishes (maybe I'll get there over time). It's just my simple blog on food and nutrition.
